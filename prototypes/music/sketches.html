Glam Gills Music Sketches

One cue for the Home Tank and one for each habitat. Every sound is made live in the browser with Web Audio, with no audio files and no downloads, so it can go straight into the game without breaking the no-network rule. Tap a cue to hear it, and switch parts on and off to find the mix you like.

How these would go into the game

  • Cues are plain data in src/logic/music.ts, keyed by habitat id, with tests, like the other game rules. A test checks that every habitat in HABITATS has a cue, so a new habitat cannot ship silent.
  • The rule: an area picks its cue by habitat, and a dusk or night area plays the dusk version of that same cue. Area 3 still sounds like the pond, just sleepier. Every habitat cue has a dusk version, made by one shared rule (slower, a music box for the tune, soft chords, no drums, fireflies), so a new dusk area never needs new music.
  • The synth that plays the cues lives in src/platform/, because it is browser glue. No new dependencies and no audio files.
  • Music starts after the first tap, which browsers require anyway. A music on/off button sits on the title screen and in the Home Tank, and the choice is saved on the device.
  • Cues cross-fade when the scene changes. Swim music stays the same when a predator warns, so music never makes a moment feel scary.
  • Requirements: PRD N6 and M1 to M12, ADR 0025, and docs/music-guide.md.
