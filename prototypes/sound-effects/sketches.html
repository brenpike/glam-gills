Open decisions, with my recommendations

The switch

Recommend "Sounds on" and "Sounds off", a speaker icon with a sparkle, on by default, kept on the device under glam-gills:sounds, outside the save, so Start over keeps it, like the music switch. It goes beside the music switch on the title screen, the pause screen and the Home Tank. "Sounds" is the word kids use; "Sound effects" is long for a 131 pixel tile.

Room in the Home Tank

Recommend one "Sound" hub tile that opens a small card with both switches. The hub already wrapped onto two rows at 1024 x 768 when the music tile came in (open question 419); a seventh tile makes it worse. On the title and pause screens there is room for the two tiles side by side. The other choice is a seventh hub tile.

Volume next to the music

Recommend the two sliders above at the same setting (both 60). The sounds are short, so they cut through at the same level without being loud. No ducking: the music never dips for a sound, so nothing changes in the music when danger is near (M5). One limiter after both keeps the loudest moment gentle (M11).

Does a warning sound count as "reacting to danger"?

Recommend the "Ripple" warning, kept soft. M5 is about the music: it sets the mood of a place, and that never changes. A warning sound is a one-off cue for one event, like the telegraph on screen, at the same level as a snack. It never repeats, builds or drones. It also helps a player who is looking at another part of the screen. If it feels tense in play-tests, drop it; the telegraph stays.

Reduced motion

Recommend no change. Reduced motion is about what moves on screen. Every sound stays the same, and no sound is ever the only sign that something happened, so the game works with sounds off.

One audio engine or two

Recommend one shared engine with two buses. One audio context, one unlock on the first input, one page-hidden pause, one reverb and one limiter, with a music bus and a sounds bus, each with its own switch and level. Two contexts would double the cost on phones, and iPhones limit how many can run. The voices (kalimba, marimba, bubble and the rest) move from musicPlayer.ts into a shared voices file both use.

Match the music's key

Recommend yes. Pitched sounds use the five notes of the playing cue's major pentatonic scale: G for Sunny Tank and Canal Paddle, F for Lily Pad Hop, D for Garden Boats. So they never clash with the tune. Turn the box off above to hear the difference in Lily Pad Hop. This is a pure rule in src/logic/ with tests.

Too many at once

Recommend a simple limit. The same sound plays at most once every 60 ms, and only one treasure fanfare plays at a time; a new one starts after the last ends. Mash the snack button to hear it.

First slice

Recommend: the shared engine, the switch, and the 12 tiles marked First slice. These are the moments a player hits every trip: menu taps, snacks, the snack ladder, finds by rarity, Tail Dash, a lost leg and its regrowth, banking, the exit opening, beating an area with stars, and the music key rule. Slice 2 is hazards and rescue; slice 3 is the Home Tank and menus.
