# Glam Gills: Find Your Flair

Glam Gills is a cozy 2D web game for kids aged 8 to 12. You play an axolotl exploring the canals of Xochimilco.
Find hidden treasures and wearables of different rarities, dodge the tilapia, and make your Home Tank and your axolotl your own.
It is mostly fun, with real axolotl facts along the way.

## What you can do now

Version 0.70.0 is playable in these habitats: the canal, the Lily pad pond, the Chinampa gardens, the Spring cave, the Deep canal and the Open lake:

- Make your own axolotl the first time you play, one step at a time in 11 steps: colour (one of eight real morphs), boy or girl (with a boy or girl sign), eyes, mouth (eight to choose from), gills, spots, tail, body, age, size, and name. Every choice is a big picture button that shows it on your axolotl, and the preview changes as you pick. On the first step, Surprise me picks a whole new axolotl at random, with a name that fits. On the other steps, it picks something new for that step only. The other seven colours are the ones you rescue.
- Type a name, or pick a suggested one that fits a boy or a girl, so typing is optional. Names stay on your device.
- On trips you swim as the axolotl you pick, in its own look and outfit. Size (Small, Medium, or Large), boy or girl, tail, body, and age are for looks only: every axolotl swims the same and has the same hit area.
- Swim with the on-screen swim pad, or the arrow keys or WASD.
- Use Tail Dash, ride the current, and eat snacks.
- Dodge three tilapia. A hit costs a leg, and legs regrow. If you lose all four, you are carried home, you lose the finds you had not banked, and the snacks start over.
- Find hidden treasures: Decorations, Wearables, and Paint shells for Dream colours. Each has a rarity badge, and hint sparkles help you look. Once revealed or found, a treasure glows faintly in its rarity's colour; it never glows while hidden.
- Each trip in Area 1 has 6 treasure slots, and chance picks each slot's rarity, so no trip is sure to have an Epic or a Legendary. Treasures you lost, and the Toy boat until you bank it, come on top of the slots. They hide in hiding spots that shuffle each trip. Rarer treasures hide nearer the tilapia, so they are harder to get.
- The snack counter shows how many snacks are left. The last 3 snacks sparkle, and snacks drift slowly.
- The exit opens when every snack is eaten. While it is closed, it shows a padlock sign and a message.
- Swim into the open exit at the far end of the canal to beat Area 1 and bank the Trip Bag. A rescued axolotl following you moves into your Home Tank.
- Earn up to 3 stars: reach the exit, find the Toy boat, and rescue the stranded axolotl or keep all four legs.
- Your best stars show on the "Area beaten" card and in the Home Tank. They are saved on your device.
- Eaten snacks come back after you are carried home or beat the area. They stay eaten after Go home.
- Bank your finds at the Home Spot to keep them safe.
- Rescue a stranded axolotl on a trip. On about 4 trips in 10, one real morph you do not have yet waits looking a little lost. Swim to it and it follows you; bring it to the Home Spot or the open exit and it moves into your Home Tank. If you meet one and do not bring it home, it waits for you on your next trip.
- Meet the tilapia: the first time you swim near one, a fact card tells you about it.
- Beat Area 1 to unlock Area 2, the Lily pad pond. It is about 5 screens wide, with 4 tilapia, 7 treasure slots, a hollow log that hides treasure, and a stream that works as the current. Its tilapia chase you when you come near, at every depth, from the surface down to the bed, then swim back to where they were. Two common carp patrol at every depth too: when one tips its nose down and puffs up mud, swim away before its tail sweeps. Some snacks swim: tadpoles swim all around their homes, and freshwater shrimp hop about their homes and flick away when you get close. "Meet the carp", "Meet the tadpole" and "Freshwater shrimp" fact cards tell you about them. It has 12 new treasures, including the Frog statue and the Lily green Dream colour.
- Beat Area 2 to unlock Area 3, the Lily pad pond at dusk. It is about 6 screens wide, with 4 tilapia, two common carp, 8 treasure slots, a root arch under the bank that works as the cave, and a slightly stronger stream. Its tilapia swim at every depth, from near the top to near the bottom. They chase you a little farther than in Area 2, then swim back to where they were. One carp swims along the bottom under the herons, so staying deep is not always safe, and the other swims near the top. More snacks swim than in Area 2: tadpoles swim all around their part of the pond, from near the top to near the bottom, a little faster, and freshwater shrimp hop about their homes and flick away a little farther when you get close. Fireflies glow in the dusk. It has 12 new treasures, including the Wooden duck decoy and the Firefly gold Dream colour.
- Watch out for the two herons in Area 3. They wade slowly in the back half of the pond and turn their heads to follow you. If one sees you close by, it freezes and pulls its neck into an S. That is your warning: swim away before it stabs, or stay deep, below its reach. A heron cannot see you when you hide in the reeds, under a lily pad, or in the root arch. A heron fact card tells you about it.
- Watch out for plastic worms in the pond. They are shiny, stiff, too-bright fake worms. If your axolotl bites one, it spits it out ("Bleh!") and feels queasy: about 60% speed for 3 seconds. It does not lose a leg, and plastic worms are never snacks. A "Plastic is not food" fact card tells you why.
- Beat Area 3 to unlock Area 4, the chinampa gardens: clear green-teal water beside garden plots, with corn, flowers and lettuce above the water and willow roots reaching into it. It is about 6 screens wide and 2 screens tall, with 4 tilapia that are a little quicker, four common carp, two punting poles, 9 treasure slots, a tangle of willow roots that hides treasure, and a current flowing through the gap between two chinampas. Treasures also hide under the overhanging banks. It has 12 new treasures, including the Scarecrow and the Poppy red Dream colour.
- Swim up and down in Area 4, the first area two screens tall: start at the top left and find the exit deep at the bottom right. The camera follows you down and back up, by the swim pad or the keys.
- Watch out for ghost nets in the chinampa gardens: torn, old fishing nets left in the water. If your axolotl swims into one, it gets snagged, wriggles free, and swims at about half speed for 2 seconds. It does not lose a leg, and swimming into another net while snagged does not slow it more or for longer. In Area 4, the two nets in the bottom row drift slowly back and forth with the current. A "Lost fishing nets" fact card tells you about them.
- Area 4 is more dangerous. Its tilapia swim after you when they see you close by, then swim back. It has four common carp: one swims from near the top down to the middle, and three swim from the middle down by the bed, so together they cover every depth. Wherever a carp swims, when it tips its nose down and puffs up mud, swim away before its tail sweeps. Bright trajinera boats float on top, and each pushes a long pole down into the water. When a boat's shadow darkens and a ripple ring shows, its pole is about to jab down, so keep out from under it. A "Meet the carp" fact card and a "Trajineras" fact card tell you about them.
- Half the snacks in Area 4 move: tadpoles swim all around their homes, and freshwater shrimp hop about, flick away when you come close, then rest.
- Beat Area 4 to unlock Area 5, the second chinampa gardens area. It is a U-turn: swim out along the top, then turn around and come back along the bottom to the exit at the left. It has 12 new treasures, including the Piñata and the Plum Dream colour, a deep plum purple.
- Watch out for the three garter snakes in Area 5. They swim near the surface, and when you come close one slides along the surface after you. Ripple rings on the water warn you where one is about to dart. Swim away or stay deep, below its reach. A "Meet garter snakes" fact card tells you about them.
- Watch out for Area 5's three tilapia: they swim at every depth, from near the top to the bottom row. When one sees you nearby, it chases you, then goes back to its loop. With three or four legs you can swim away, and Tail Dash always gets away.
- Watch out for Area 5's five common carp. They are meaner than Area 4's carp: their warning is a little shorter, and their tails sweep faster and reach farther. They swim at every depth, the bottom row too, and chase you when you come close. When one tips its nose down and puffs up mud, swim away before its tail sweeps.
- Watch out for the punting poles in Area 5. Four trajinera boats float at the surface, and each one jabs its long pole straight down, deep into the bottom row, every 4 seconds. When a boat's shadow darkens and its pole's tip dips to the water with a ripple ring, a jab is coming: get out from under it. A touch costs a leg. A "Trajineras" fact card tells you about them.
- More of Area 5's snacks move than Area 2's: 10 tadpoles wander around in loops, faster than Area 2's, and 9 freshwater shrimp hop from spot to spot, then flick backward when you get close, rest and flick again.
- Water hyacinth mats float in Area 5. Each mat opens and closes on an 8 second cycle. A closed mat blocks the way, and a mat never traps you: wait for it to open, or swim around. A "Water hyacinth" fact card tells you about them.
- Beat Area 5 to unlock Area 6, the spring cave: cold, clear spring water that opens to the sky at the top and gets darker the deeper you go. It is the first descent, about 5 screens wide and 3 screens tall: start at the top left, swim right along the top, down and back left along the middle, then down again and right along the bottom, against the gentle flow of the spring, to the exit deep in the dark at the bottom right. It has 11 treasure slots, rock ledges and a grotto on the bottom that hide treasure, and no tilapia.
- Watch out for the three crayfish in Area 6. They walk along the bottom, each on its own stretch: one where it is light and two in the dark. When you come close, a crayfish stops and opens its claws wide. That is your warning: swim up and away before it pinches. A crayfish chases you only along its own stretch of the bottom, and its pinch reaches only along the bottom, so the water above it is safe. A "Meet the crayfish" fact card tells you about them.
- Watch out for Area 6's six common carp. The carp are a little meaner than Area 5's: their warning is a little shorter, and their tails sweep faster and reach farther. They swim at every depth and chase you when you come close; when one tips its nose down and puffs up mud, swim away before its tail sweeps.
- Watch out for the four stalactites and the geyser vent in Area 6. The stalactites hang from the rock ledges in the top row, and each drips a stream of water down every 4 seconds. When a drop swells at a stalactite's tip and ripple rings spread under it, get out from under it. The geyser vent sits on the floor of the bottom row and bursts a column of water up about every 5 seconds. When bubbles rise from its mouth with a shimmer ring, swim clear; the water above the column is safe. A touch costs a leg. A "Stalactites" fact card and a "Geysers" fact card tell you about them.
- The bottom of the spring cave is dark: in the right half of the bottom row, you cannot see far. Your axolotl glows softly, so you always see what comes close. Snacks and treasures hide in the dark until light reaches them, but the crayfish, the carp, their warnings and the exit always show, and the exit glows faintly. Six glow-plants grow in the dark: touch one to light it up, and it lights the water around it for the rest of the trip. If you swim in the dark for 20 seconds without lighting a new one, the nearest glow-plant you have not lit pulses to show you the way (with reduced motion, it glows brighter instead).
- Most of Area 6's snacks move: 24 of its 36. 13 tadpoles wander around in loops, a little faster than Area 5's, and 11 freshwater shrimp hop from spot to spot, then flick backward a little faster when you get close, rest and flick again. Some of them are in the dark.
- Area 6 has 13 new treasures, including the Fossil ammonite, the Moonstone Dream colour (a pale silvery white) and the Crystal shine Dream colour, a Legendary shimmery colour hidden deep and near danger. The Rare, Epic and Legendary ones hide in the dark: under the rock ledge over a crayfish, in the grotto, and just past the spring's flow, and the Legendary ones are in a Dash pot or in the open.
- Beat Area 6 to unlock Area 7, the second spring cave area. It is the first climb, about 8 screens wide and 2 screens tall: start at the bottom left, swim right along the bottom, ride up past a vent, then swim right along the top under the rock ledges to the exit at the top right. It has 12 treasure slots, a small dark stretch with three glow-plants, and no tilapia.
- Watch out for the three pulsing vents in Area 7. Each vent blows across your way on a cycle: calm, then bubbles build at its mouth, then a strong surge of water pushes you back out. The later vents rest for less time. Swim across while a vent is calm. A surge never costs a leg, and it never pushes you into a crayfish or a wall. An "Underwater springs" fact card tells you about them.
- A big round pebble blocks a side room at the bottom right, walled in with solid rock. Swim into the pebble from outside and it rolls slowly into the room, one way only, so it can never get stuck. If you stay near it for 10 seconds without moving it, an arrow shows you which way to push. The pebble is back in its place on every trip. The room holds the Rare treasures, the Ancient clay pot among them, and a grotto with an Epic one. You never need the room to reach the exit.
- Area 7 keeps the spring cave's dangers, a little meaner. Three crayfish walk the bottom, and they rest less between pinches and walk a little faster. Six common carp swim at every depth, with a shorter warning and a longer, faster tail sweep. Six stalactites drip from the rock ledges and two geyser vents burst up from the floor.
- Most of Area 7's snacks move: 27 of its 40. 12 tadpoles wander around in loops, and 15 freshwater shrimp hop from spot to spot.
- Area 7 has 13 new treasures, including the Ancient clay pot, the Chocolate Dream colour (a deep chocolate brown) and the Glowworm Dream colour, a Legendary patterned colour with glowing dots, hidden deep and near danger, in a Dash pot or in the open. The Epic and Legendary ones hide just past the spring's flow, and one Epic hides in the grotto in the side room.
- Beat Area 7 to unlock Area 8, the deep canal: deep, murky water split into two lanes by an old-stone wall. It has 13 treasure slots, 2 tilapia, 5 common carp and 2 black bass, the deep canal's predator, which swim the lanes; a bass cannot sense, chase or strike through the wall or other rock, so rock is a safe place to hide. Two gliding trajinera boats drift along the surface and push you aside, but a push never costs a leg. Watch out for the four punting poles too. A "Meet the black bass" fact card tells you about the bass.
- Area 8 has 13 new treasures, including the Jade and Fiesta shine Dream colours and 5 new Wearables with perks.
- Beat Area 8 to unlock Area 9, the second deep canal area and the first hub: about 9 screens wide and 2 screens tall, with the Home Spot in the middle and a branch on each side. The left branch has the sunken hull cave and a side-canal inflow. The way to the exit goes right, along the surface under the moored boats, through two murky patches, under both anchors and past both bailing boats, to the exit low at the end of the right branch. It has 14 treasure slots.
- Watch out for Area 9's three patches of murky, dirty water. Swimming through costs nothing at first, but while you stay in, a little ring meter over your axolotl fills up. If it fills, you lose a leg. Swim out into clean water for 1 second and the meter empties. A "Dirty water" fact card tells you about it.
- Two boats in Area 9 bail out water with a bucket on a rope. When the bucket's shadow and ripples show on the water, it is about to dip: if it dips onto you, it scoops you up and tosses you back toward the Home Spot. A toss never costs a leg.
- Area 9's dangers are a little meaner than Area 8's: 2 tilapia, 6 common carp and 2 black bass. They patrol a little faster and rest less between strikes, and the tilapia and the bass strike faster. Watch out for the two anchors too, which boaters drop on a chain from the surface to the canal bed and pull back up, and the two mud seeps, which send a column of bubbles up from the canal floor. When two tall chain links show, an anchor is coming; when loose bits of silt show, a seep is about to bubble. A touch costs a leg. An "Anchors" fact card and a "Mud bubbles" fact card tell you about them.
- Most of Area 9's snacks move: 33 of its 48. 16 tadpoles wander around in loops, and 17 freshwater shrimp hop from spot to spot.
- Area 9 has 13 new treasures, including the Little bailing bucket, the Olive and Lantern shine Dream colours and 5 new Wearables with perks.
- Beat Area 9 to unlock Area 10, the third deep canal area and the first grand tour: a big trip 10 screens wide and 3 screens tall. Start at the top left, swim the upper of two lanes split by an old-stone wall, turn around along the top, swim down through all three rows and right along the bottom, then climb back up to a fork at the top right. Down from the fork is the exit, low at the right end; the other way leads past a side-canal inflow to hidden treasure. It has 15 treasure slots.
- Watch out for the fishing trajineras in Area 10. Three boats drift slowly along the surface, each hanging worms on lines. A lure looks just like a snack worm, but if your axolotl eats one, it loses a leg. Look up for the line running straight up to the boat, with a little float bead at the water line. Touching a line or a boat is safe. After a tug the lure is pulled up for 5 seconds, and it comes back only once you have swum away from it. A lure is never a snack. A "Fishing lines" fact card tells you about them.
- Watch out for the two drag nets in Area 10. Each hangs between two boats across your way. When its floats rise and its boats lean (with reduced motion, the floats light up), the net is about to sweep across. If it sweeps over you, you lose a leg, even during Tail Dash. Each net has two safe hollows, darker pockets the net never covers: wait in one, or swim out of the net's way before it reaches you. A "Fishing nets" fact card tells you about them.
- Area 10 mixes the tilapia with every earlier predator: 2 tilapia, 10 common carp, a black bass, a heron, a garter snake and a crayfish, each guarding its own part of the tour. Watch out for its five anchors, six mud seeps and two murky patches too.
- Most of Area 10's snacks move: 36 of its 52. 18 tadpoles wander around in loops, and 18 freshwater shrimp hop from spot to spot.
- Area 10 has 13 new treasures, including the Axolotl statue, the Peach and Confetti shine Dream colours and 5 new Wearables with perks.
- Beat Area 10 to unlock Area 11, the fourth deep canal area, at golden hour. It is a U-turn 10 screens wide and 2 tall. Start at the top left, swim east along the top row, down in the last column, then west along the bottom row to the exit, low in the first column, under the Home Spot. It has 16 treasure slots.
- Watch out for the three Neotropic cormorants in Area 11. Each floats on the surface, then dives down and back up in a U-shaped arc. A hit costs a leg. The main path stays deep where a cormorant could dive, so swim deep or away to stay safe. A "Meet the cormorant" fact card tells you about them.
- Area 11 has a warm golden-hour look: papel picado banners, marigold petals (a few sink slowly), water hyacinth rafts, ahuejote root curtains and chinampa walls, sunken trajinera arches, warm light rays and a lit lower row. It plays the dusk version of the trajinera waltz.
- Area 11 has 14 treasures: the Little cormorant; the Marigold, Golden, Lime, Hyacinth and Lagoon shine Dream colours; 3 new Decorations (the Flower raft, the Festival kite and the Carousel); and 5 new Wearables with perks (the Propeller cap, the Star sunglasses, the Robot suit, the Jetpack and the Wizard hat). It has 6 new hiding spots, so a trip leaves about 5.6 of its 16 slots empty.
- Beat Area 11 to unlock Area 12, the open lake, the first area outside the canals. It is a bowl 9 screens wide and 2 tall, with stepped lakebed shelves and two depth bands. Area 12 has 12 treasures of its own, all Common or Rare: the Little pelican; 4 more Decorations (the Windsock, the Pinwheel, the Twin volcanoes and the Little sailboat); the Tule green and Obsidian Dream colours; and 5 new Wearables with perks (the Wave socks, the Cloud scarf, the Pelican swim ring, the Surfboard and the Pelican slippers).
- Watch out for the pelican line in Area 12: a line of American white pelicans that float on the surface. Each one warns first, then they dip their bills together. A scoop costs a leg and bumps your axolotl away. It is never a catch. A "Meet the pelican" fact card tells you about them.
- Wind and waves blow across the open lake. Surface gusts switch direction and fade with depth, and a ripple warns you before a gust. Swim deeper to get out of the wind.
- The open lake has its own look: open sky, the volcanoes Popocatépetl and Iztaccíhuatl, water that gets darker with each depth band, and silt-and-stone lakebed terraces. It plays its own music, "Lagoon Breeze".
- Beat Area 12 to unlock Area 13, the open lake's trench: a stepped dive 10 screens wide and 3 tall, down to the first pitch-black bottom. Touch a fading light to light the dark; it fades slowly, so touch it again. Swim away from the mud turtles on the bottom: each creeps, warns, then snaps. A "Meet the mud turtle" fact card tells you about them. Area 13 hides treasures of its own, the rarest deepest in the dark pit: the Little mud turtle, the Pitch black and Trench shine Dream colours, Decorations such as the Little lighthouse and the Diving bell, and Wearables with perks such as the Diving goggles and the Mini submarine, a big item for the back.
- Beat Area 13 to unlock Area 14, the twin trenches, on the open lake in the late afternoon: two pitch-black pits side by side with a shallow ridge between them. You start on the ridge, and the exit is deep in the dark on the floor of the far pit. Watch out for sharp litter on the pit floors: touching a dented can or a chipped bottle costs a leg. Reed islands drift slowly across the surface, and a pelican never dips its bill where an island floats, so swim under one when the pelicans scoop. A "Litter in the water" fact card tells you about the litter.
- Listen to cozy, happy background music, made right in the game with no downloads. Each habitat has its own tune, Area 3 plays a sleepy dusk version of the pond's, and the deep canal plays the trajinera waltz. It starts after your first tap or key press, plays softer while you pause a trip, and stops when you switch to another tab. Turn it off or on with the Music switch on the title screen, in the Home Tank, or on the pause screen; the game remembers your choice on this device, even after Start over.
- Visit your Home Tank after the title and after every trip. A trip-end message tells you how the trip went.
- Watch your axolotl swim around the Home Tank. With reduced motion it stays still.
- Rename and restyle your axolotl from the Home Tank: tap it, or move the focus to it (its name shows) and press Enter. A card with its name opens, offering Restyle (starts on Eyes), Outfit (opens the Outfit tab) and, once you have one, Dream colour (opens the Dream colour tab). Restyling uses the same step screens, with tabs to jump between steps, plus a Dream colour tab and an Outfit tab. Every step shows the outfit you are choosing. Its colour and boy or girl show but cannot change. Cancel leaves without keeping the changes.
- Give your axolotls Dream colours: on the restyle screen's Dream colour tab, put any Dream colour you banked on any axolotl, or pick Real colour to take it off. One Dream colour can be on lots of axolotls at once, and spots show on top of it. Dream colours are not found in nature. Each one is held by its own Paint shell, and there are more to find as you reach new areas.
- Dress your axolotls in outfits on the restyle screen's Outfit tab: eight slots (Head, Neck, Face, Shirt, Back, Tail, Socks, and Feet), one Wearable you banked in each. Each Wearable gives one small perk on trips: Speed, Regrowth, Dash (a longer Tail Dash), Shell (blocks one hit per trip), Treasure sense (hint sparkles from further away), or Snack magnet (pulls nearby snacks to you). Each perk shows with an icon and words, and each perk type has a cap. Only the axolotl you take exploring uses its perks. A lost leg's sock and shoe come back when the leg has fully regrown. There are lots of Wearables to find. Some Wearables are big: the Propeller cap and the Wizard hat sit on a larger head, and the Jetpack, the Surfboard and the Mini submarine sit on the back, with matching footprints.
- Press Go exploring to pick which axolotl goes on the trip. The card shows each axolotl in its outfit and the chosen one's perks, and Change outfit takes you to its Outfit tab first. The game remembers your pick. Go! then opens the area map.
- Pick where to explore on the area map: each area shows your best stars, a lock if you cannot play it yet, and "New!" when you just unlocked it. Replay any area you have beaten. Beat an area to unlock the next one. The map has six habitat sections, each on its own coloured panel with a tab naming the habitat and its areas, such as "Canal: Area 1" and "Deep canal: Areas 8 to 11". A habitat you have not reached yet still shows its name.
- Decorate your Home Tank with the Decorations you banked, by touch or keyboard. A Decoration stands on the gravel, floats on the water surface, or hangs from the glass top; the ones that float or hang move only left and right. In the canal, all Decorations stand on the gravel. In the pond, the Lily pad and the Lotus lantern float on the surface, and the Cattail clump and the Frog statue stand on the gravel. In the chinampa gardens, the Floating gourd floats on the surface, the Papel picado banner, the Willow swing and the Piñata hang from the glass top, and the other seven stand on the gravel. In the spring cave, the Glow moss ball floats on the surface, the Crystal wind chime and the Glowing stalactite hang from the glass top, and the other nine stand on the gravel. In the deep canal, the Paper boat, the Little trajinera and the Floating flower market float on the surface, the Flower garland hangs from the glass top, and the Painted oar and the Trajinera flower arch stand on the gravel. From Area 9, the Floating cork, the Paper lantern boat and the Floating music stage float on the surface, the Painted name sign hangs from the glass top, and the Clay jug and the Little bailing bucket stand on the gravel. From Area 10, the Floating candle and the Marigold boat float on the surface, the Festival flag string hangs from the glass top, and the Axolotl statue, the Mariachi band figures and the Refuge snow globe stand on the gravel. From Area 11, the Little cormorant and the Flower raft float on the surface, the Festival kite hangs from the glass top, and the Carousel stands on the gravel. In the open lake, the Little pelican and the Little sailboat float on the surface, the Windsock hangs from the glass top, and the Pinwheel and the Twin volcanoes stand on the gravel. From Area 13, the Lake buoy floats on the surface, the Raincloud mobile and the Glow-flower lamp hang from the glass top, and the Stone stack, the Little lighthouse, the Diving bell, the Little mud turtle and the Fading light garden stand on the gravel. There are lots of Decorations to find. On small screens some Home Tank buttons become icon tiles, and a caption names the one you are on.
- Open the Treasure Chest to see every Decoration, Wearable, and Dream colour. The ones you banked show in full; the ones you have not found yet show as silhouettes with their rarity and a hint naming the habitat and area they are in and where to look, such as "Lily pad pond: Area 3: look in a cave or log, or past the current!". Each section has a line saying what its items are for. Each Wearable you banked shows its slot and its perk, with the perk's icon. Tap the Filter button (the funnel) to show only some treasures: pick types, Found or Not found, rarities, and areas, as many as you like. Each area shows how many of its treasures you have found, or "All found!", so you can see where treasure is still hiding. Show all clears the filter. The chest remembers your filter until you reload the game.
- The first Decoration, Wearable, and Paint shell you bank each get a one-time card after the trip. The Paint shell card's "Try it now!" opens the Dream colour tab.
- Visit the Xochimilco refuge from the Home Tank's Refuge button: a safe home for axolotls, based on real conservation work in Xochimilco, with one section for each habitat, the deep canal and the open lake included. Each area you beat clears its trash from the refuge. The next time you open the refuge, the trash floats away with a sparkle, and once every area of a habitat is beaten, its section turns clean, with plants growing back and axolotls resting. A badge on the Refuge button shows when there is a clean-up to see. A section you have not reached yet shows "???" and the area that opens it, and "More to come" waits at the end.
- Celebrate each habitat you clean up: back in the Home Tank, an "All clean!" card names your explorer and the habitat. Press See it again under a clean section in the refuge to see its card again.
- Find two fact cards in the refuge: "Axolotl refuges", the first time you visit, and "Canal clean-ups", once you have cleared some trash.
- Open the Collection Book to see the morphs you rescued, with a fun fact for each, the Dream colours, and the fact cards, such as the tilapia card, the "Plastic is not food" card and the "Boy or girl?" card.
- Your progress saves on your device by itself: your axolotls, treasures, Home Tank and stars. There is no save button.
- Start over from the title screen to erase everything and make a new axolotl. It asks twice before it erases anything.
  Grown-ups: progress is saved only in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data erases it.

- Node.js `>=20.19.0` (the repo pins 20.20.2 in `.nvmrc`)
- npm

```sh
nvm use
npm install
```

## Scripts

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `npm run dev` | Start the Vite dev server with hot reload. |
| `npm run build` | Type-check (`tsc --noEmit`) and build to `dist/`. |
| `npm run preview` | Serve the production build locally. |
| `npm test` | Run the unit tests once (Vitest). |
| `npm run test:watch` | Run the unit tests in watch mode. |
| `npm run lint` | Lint the project (ESLint). |
| `npm run typecheck` | Type-check only. |

### Dev-only flags

Add these to the address that `npm run dev` prints, for example `http://localhost:5173/?perf&renderer=canvas`.
They work only on the dev server, never in the build (`npm run build`, `npm run preview`, or the live site). Flags combine.

| Flag | What it does |
|---|---|
| `?perf` | Shows a frame-time overlay (live p50 and p95 frame time, p95 axolotl drawing time, and texture bakes) and logs a p50/p95 summary to the console every 30 s of frames. Stores and sends nothing. |
| `?compare` | Runs only the rig compare view: the reference `drawAxolotl` on the left, the axolotl rig on the right, in the same pose. Keys: `x` `t` `b` `a` `z` `g` `e` `u` `s` cycle the look, `m` the morph, `d` the Dream colour, `o` the full outfit, `l` the leg state; `h` Tail Dash, `p` pause, `n` step, `r` still pose, `v` overlay the rig on the reference at 50%. Shift steps back. The on-screen legend lists them all; see [`src/scenes/RigCompareScene.ts`](src/scenes/RigCompareScene.ts). |
| `?area=<id>` | Loads a test area instead of the area map. For example, `?area=dev-test-area` loads a 6 x 2 screen test area for play-testing and the frame-time check, and `?area=dev-danger-area` loads a 6 x 2 screen chinampa area with every danger part and mobile snack. Absent from the production build. |
| `?decor` | Adds three test Decorations to the Home Tank: a lily pad that floats, a firefly jar that hangs, and a wide string of lights that hangs. They are yours for this page session only: never saved and never in the Treasure Chest. Absent from the production build. |
| `?renderer=canvas` | Forces Phaser's Canvas renderer instead of WebGL. |

On a phone they need the dev server, which is harder to reach on WSL2 (see [Testing on a phone](#testing-on-a-phone)).

## Testing on a phone

The easiest way is the live build on GitHub Pages: <https://brenpike.github.io/glam-gills/>. It updates after CI passes on `main`.

The dev server listens on all network interfaces (`vite --host` behavior), so a phone on the same Wi-Fi can open it.
On WSL2 this needs Windows port forwarding to the WSL VM, so the Pages build is the more reliable path.

## Project structure

| Path | Contents |
|---|---|
| `src/main.ts` | Entry point; creates the Phaser game. |
| `src/logic/` | Pure game rules with headless unit tests. No Phaser or DOM. |
| `src/art/` | Canvas 2D drawing code, baked into Phaser textures. |
| `src/scenes/` | Phaser scenes that connect logic, art, and input. |
| `docs/` | Tech stack, ADRs, PRDs, the art style guide, the music guide, and the sound guide. |
| `prototypes/art-styles/` | Standalone art-style prototypes. |
| `prototypes/music/` | Standalone background music sketches. |
| `prototypes/sound-effects/` | Standalone sound effects sketches. |
| `.github/workflows/` | CI and GitHub Pages deploy. |
