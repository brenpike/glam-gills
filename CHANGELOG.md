# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

## [0.55.1] - 2026-10-29

### Fixed

- A shrimp that cannot flick away no longer makes the game redo work every frame (#139).

## [0.55.0] - 2026-10-29

### Changed

- The spring cave's dark water now fades softly into the water round it instead of ending in a hard black edge.
- Glow-plants have a thin dark edge, so you can still spot them where the darkness fades.

## [0.54.1] - 2026-10-28

### Fixed

- Area 7's exit gate now stands where the exit really is, at the top right, on the pebble room's rock roof. Before, the gate was drawn on the cave floor inside the pebble room, and swimming into it did nothing (#183).

## [0.54.0] - 2026-10-28

### Changed

- Crayfish now pinch up at you if you hover over them. When a crayfish opens its claws, swim away.
- Carp in the Spring cave sweep faster, and a sweep hits as soon as it touches you.
- Stalactite drips and geyser columns are wider, reach farther and stay out longer.
- Area 6 has more dangers along the way: one more carp, one more stalactite and one more geyser vent.

## [0.53.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added

- Area 7, the second Spring cave area. It is 8 screens wide and 2 screens tall, and you climb up through the cave to find the exit at the top right (#105).
- Watch out for pulsing vents. A vent goes calm, then bubbles build at its mouth, then it blasts a strong current that pushes you back. Swim across while it is calm. A vent never costs a leg (#105).
- A big round pebble blocks a side room. Swim into it from outside to roll it out of the way. If you stay near it for a while, an arrow shows you which way to push (#105).
- The side room has solid rock walls, and it hides the special treasure, the Ancient clay pot (#105).
- Crayfish, carp, stalactites and geyser vents are back in Area 7, and the crayfish walk a little faster (#105).
- 12 new treasures to find in Area 7, including the Ancient clay pot and the Chocolate Dream colour (#105).
- A new fact card: "Underwater springs" (#105).
- The refuge's Spring cave section now has trash from Area 7 too (#105).

## [0.52.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added

- Area 6, the Spring cave, is a new habitat with its own place on the area map and its own music. It is 5 screens wide and 3 screens tall, and you swim down into the cave to find the exit (#77).
- Crayfish walk the cave floor. A crayfish opens its claws wide before it pinches, so swim up and away (#77).
- The bottom of the cave is dark. Your axolotl glows a little, and glow-plants light up when you touch them and stay lit for the rest of the trip. If you are stuck in the dark, the nearest glow-plant pulses (#77).
- Watch out for two new cave dangers. Stalactites on the cave roof drip a stream of water down, and geyser vents on the cave floor burst a column of water up. A drop swells on a stalactite and bubbles rise from a vent before they go, so you have time to swim clear (#77).
- 12 new treasures to find in Area 6, including the Fossil ammonite (the special treasure) and the Moonstone Dream colour (#77).
- Three new fact cards: "Meet the crayfish", "Stalactites" and "Geysers" (#77).
- The refuge has a Spring cave section (#77).

## [0.50.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added

- A predator's warning now plays a soft ripple, once when its warning starts. A punting pole on screen plays the same ripple when its warning starts, and only one ripple plays at a time (#166).
- Being carried home plays a lullaby, after the last leg's "uh-oh" has rung out (#166).
- The hazards have sounds. A ghost net plays a twang when it snags you and a wriggle when you get free, a plastic worm plays a "bleh", the Shell perk plays a sound when it blocks a hit, and hyacinth mats on screen play a rustle and a boing as they open (#166).
- Swimming into the closed exit plays two soft "not yet" boops (#166).
- Catching a tadpole or shrimp plays the snack's gulp and note with a small boing, and climbs the snack scale like any snack. A shrimp flicking away plays a tiny flick (#166).
- Tail Dash plays a bloop when it is ready again. Bumping a sealed pot plays a clay knock, and breaking a Dash pot plays a "tok", a crumble and bubbles (#166).
- A Paint shell plays a shimmer after its find sound (#166).
- Meeting a stranded axolotl plays a rescue sound, and banking a rescued axolotl plays a happy "safe" sound after the bank sound (#166).

## [0.49.0] - 2026-10-27

### Added

- Your trip now has sounds. Eating a snack plays a soft gulp and a note, and snacks eaten close together climb the scale, one note each, up to 10 notes. Each of the last 3 snacks plays a high note with a twinkle (#165).
- Finding a treasure plays a sound for its rarity, Common, Rare, Epic or Legendary, one find at a time (#165).
- A Tail Dash plays a whoosh when it starts. Pressing the Dash button while the Dash recharges plays nothing, and the Dash button no longer taps (#165).
- Losing a leg plays a kind "uh-oh", and each leg growing back plays a regrowth sound (#165).
- Eating every snack plays a "ta-da" once the exit opens, banking your finds plays one bubble and note for each find, up to 5, then a ukulele strum, and the tank arrival card plays a win for the stars you earned when you beat an area (#165).

### Changed

- Sounds asked for at the same moment now take turns. Snacks eaten at the same moment play one after another, so the scale still climbs and you hear every last-snack twinkle, and two treasures of the same rarity found together both play their sound. The same sound played again very quickly now plays a moment later instead of being skipped, and fast button taps never pile up (#165).

## [0.47.2] - 2026-10-27

### Changed

- Lost legs now take about 40 seconds to grow back instead of about 20, so a hit matters for longer. Each snack you eat while a leg is missing still saves about 4 seconds, now a tenth of a leg instead of a fifth. The Regrowth perk still cuts the time by its percent.

## [0.47.1] - 2026-10-27

### Fixed

- On phones, the music and sounds now start on your first tap. Before, a phone ignored the game's request to start sound when your finger first touched the screen, so the music stayed silent, even in the character creator and the Home Tank, until you turned the music switch off and on again. The game now keeps asking until the sound is really playing (#170).

## [0.47.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added

- A sounds switch, "Sounds on" or "Sounds off" with a speaker icon, beside the music switch on the title screen and the pause screen. Sounds are on at first launch, and your choice is kept on the device, also after Start over. Turning sounds on plays two little notes so you can hear them; turning them off is silent (#164).
- Every button press now plays a soft tap, a little bubble and wood tick, whether you tap it, click it or press Enter or Space on it. Moving the keyboard focus and typing a name stay silent (#164).

### Changed

- In the Home Tank, the music tile is now a "Sound" tile. It opens a small card with the music switch and the sounds switch. The hub is narrower, so at 1024 x 768 its buttons fit on one row again (#164).
- The music and the sounds share one audio engine, with a bus each, one reverb and one limiter. The music sounds the same as before (#164).
