# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

## [0.33.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added

- On the area map, each habitat sits on its own lightly coloured panel with an outline. A tab names the habitat and its areas, such as "Lily pad pond: Areas 2 and 3" (#113).
- In the refuge, each habitat shows which areas it covers, such as "Areas 2 and 3" under "Lily pad pond". A "???" section shows no areas (#113).

### Changed

- Treasure Chest hints name the habitat first, then the area, such as "Lily pad pond: Area 3: look in a cave or log, or past the current!" (#113).

## [0.32.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added

- The shared parts for a harder danger pass are ready. The shipped areas do not change yet: everything below shows only in the dev area for now, tadpoles and shrimp included (#117).
- Predators that chase you on a leash: they follow you for a while, then let go (#117).
- The common carp, with its own fact card and art (#117).
- Trajinera punting poles that jab down into the water and cost a leg (#117).
- Ghost nets that drift slowly through the water (#117).
- Tadpoles that swim along short lanes, with a fact card and art (#117).

## [0.31.0] - 2026-10-23

### Added

- Area 5, the second Chinampa gardens area, is open. It is a U-turn: swim out along the top, then turn around and come back along the bottom to the exit (#103).
- Garter snakes swim near the surface of Area 5. Ripple rings on the water warn you before a snake comes. Swim away or stay deep to be safe (#103).
- Water hyacinth mats float in Area 5. Each mat opens and closes on an 8 second cycle. A closed mat blocks the way, but a mat never traps you (#103).
- Two new fact cards: "Meet garter snakes" and "Water hyacinth" (#103).
- 12 new treasures to find in Area 5, including the Piñata (the special treasure) and the Plum Dream colour, the 16th Dream colour (#103).
- Six new looks for your axolotl to wear (#103).

### Changed

- The Treasure Chest now lists 72 treasures: 25 Decorations, 31 Wearables and 16 Paint shells (#103).
- The arrow on the exit sign now points the way out. In Area 5 it points left (#103).
- The chinampa section of the refuge has a second batch of trash to clear (#103).
- Area 5's tilapia strike faster (#103).
- On a small portrait screen, Outfit choices show one choice a page (#103).

## [0.30.1] - 2026-10-23

### Changed

- Two herons now stand and wade in the back of Area 3, the Lily pad pond. When you swim close near the surface, a heron freezes and pulls its neck into an S, then stabs fast at where you were. Swim away while it is an S to dodge it, swim deep, or hide in the reeds, under a lily pad or in the root arch, where a heron cannot see you (#111).
- Stranded axolotls turn up a little more often, on about 4 trips in 10 (#111).
- The source for the heron's fact card was checked again (#111).

### Fixed

- When an area's common treasures are all found, a treasure slot no longer jumps to a much rarer treasure, so a trip can no longer fill up with Legendaries (#111).

## [0.25.0] - 2026-10-22

### Changed

- Trip treasures: each trip in Area 1 now has 6 treasure slots. Each slot picks its rarity by chance: Common 65%, Rare 27%, Epic 6%, Legendary 2%. No trip has a guaranteed Epic or Legendary. When a rarity has nothing left, the slot takes the next more common rarity, then the next rarer one. Lost treasures and the special treasure come on top of the slots (#94).
- Area 1's special treasure is now the Toy boat (Rare) instead of the Castle tower (Epic). Star 2 in Area 1 now needs the Toy boat. Stars already earned are kept, so a player who earned Star 2 with the Castle tower keeps it (#93).
- A stranded axolotl now appears on about 3 trips in 10 (30%) in every area. One you met but did not bring home still waits for your next trip (#94).

- Floating items bob gently and Hanging items sway. With reduced motion on, they stay still, like the Pond plant (#95).
- Wide Hanging items, such as string lights, slide along the top of the tank (#95).

- On small screens, Decorate, Treasure Chest, Collection Book and Put away are smaller 48x48 icon buttons, with a caption naming the focused one. The tray squares get smaller and Done gets narrower (#95).
- On large screens the hub sits in the open water below the top band (#95).
- Saves are unchanged (#95).

## [0.11.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added

- Character creator on first play: pick your axolotl's name, eyes, mouth, gills, spots, size and colour, with a live preview, Surprise me and Done. The colour is one of the four real colours, shown with its name and badge, and Surprise me picks one too. It starts with a suggested name and the Leucistic colour, so Done works at once. - Size is for looks only: small, medium and large axolotls have the same hit area and the same speed.
- Your axolotl is drawn with its own look on trips and in the Home Tank.

### Added

- Invasive tilapia patrol the canal. They never enter the start area. Every lunge is telegraphed with a wind-up pose, a "!" sign, and a glint, so it is readable without colour and under reduced motion. A cooldown follows each lunge.
- Leg loss and regrowth. A connecting lunge removes one of the axolotl's four legs with a sparkle poof, and a steady stretch of protected time follows. Legs regrow one at a time after about 20 s.
- Legs display in the HUD. Each leg shows as present, regrowing (with progress), or lost, by shape and fill.
- Gentle trip end. Losing the fourth leg floats the axolotl home in a bubble. A kind "Carried home!" card offers "Swim again" with four legs. Snacks are kept.
- Update check. The title screen offers "A new version is ready!" with a one-tap Update. It checks only the game's own site, sends no player data, stays silent when offline, and never touches saved progress (see ADR 0009).
- Version and build shown in small text on the title screen.
- Art style guide in `docs/art-style-guide.md`: provisional MVP visuals for rarity badges, the tilapia and its telegraph, leg states, the protected ring, the Home Spot burrow, the Dash pot, the hint sparkle, the Home Tank kit, Wearable slots with keep-clear zones, creator variants, and 12 Dream colours.

### Changed

- Swim speed and wobble now scale with leg count. One leg still beats every current.
- Tail Dash works at any leg count.
